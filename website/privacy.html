Privacy

We treat your information with care.

This statement explains what we collect through this website, why we use it and the choices available to you.

Last updated 3 November 2026

What we collect

If you contact us through the enquiry form, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, organisation, role and message. Please do not include names or sensitive information about individual children in the form.

How we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to you, arrange a conversation and understand how people want to explore See My Mind. We do not sell your personal information.

Who processes it

The enquiry form is submitted through Web3Forms, which delivers the message to our designated email recipient. Their service may process the submitted information to provide that delivery. We only ask for information needed to respond to your enquiry.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies on this website. We use essential browser storage to remember that you have acknowledged the cookie notice, so it is not shown on every visit. You can clear this storage through your browser settings.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, request correction or deletion, or ask us to stop using it for a particular purpose. To make a request, email hello@seemymind.co.uk.

Questions

If you have a question about this statement or how we handle information, please contact us at hello@seemymind.co.uk. We will update this statement when our website or information practices change.
