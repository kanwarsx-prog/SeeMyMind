Your experience

What would you like us to know?

You can share as much or as little as feels comfortable. Please do not include names or sensitive information about individual children.

Your story will be reviewed by the See My Mind team. We will only contact you if you provide an email address.

A note on privacy

You are in control of what you share.

Please avoid including names, diagnoses, school names or other details that could identify a child. If you choose to share your story publicly, we may edit it lightly for clarity while preserving its meaning.
