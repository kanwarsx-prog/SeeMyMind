About us

The people behind See My Mind.

See My Mind brings together lived experience, enterprise technology, software engineering and commercial expertise to turn an important idea into a practical product.

Different experience. One shared ambition.

Built by people who understand the problem from different sides.

Our founding team combines the parent perspective, the classroom problem, the architecture and the engineering needed to build something schools and families can genuinely use.

Shy El-Ghazi

Founder & CEO

Shy created See My Mind after living the problem as a parent. She leads the vision, partnerships and growth, keeping the product grounded in the real experiences of children, families and schools.

Shuaib Rabbani

Co-Founder, Technology Architect & Business Development

Shuaib brings more than three decades of enterprise technology and platform architecture experience, alongside personal experience as the parent of an autistic child.

Sushil Kanwar

Co-Founder & Technology Lead

Sushil leads software engineering, application development, UI and UX, platform integration and the intelligence capabilities that will sit within See My Mind.

Why this team

Lived experience gives the work its purpose. Engineering makes it usable.

Shy brings the original vision and understanding of the problem. Shuaib brings enterprise architecture, commercial technology experience and business development. Sushil brings the engineering capability to build and evolve the platform.

Together, we are focused on creating something meaningful for children and families, practical for schools and capable of growing into a sustainable product.

Building with people

We are developing See My Mind with the people who will use it.

That means listening to parents and carers, teachers, TAs, SENCOs, school leaders and other professionals, testing assumptions early and letting real-world experience shape the product.

The goal is not to build software and ask people to adapt to it. The goal is to build See My Mind around the way children are actually supported and the information people genuinely need.
