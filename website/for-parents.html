For parents & carers

You know your child. Help school see what you see.

See My Mind helps families share the context school cannot see and build a fuller understanding together with teachers, TAs and SENCOs.

Tell your school about See My Mind

Home and school

Your child should not have to be two different people.

A difficult night, a change in routine or something your child is worried about can affect the whole school day. A strategy that works brilliantly at home may never reach the classroom.

See My Mind connects those perspectives so parents, carers and school staff can build a fuller understanding together.

Your child’s strengths matter

Achievements, interests and positive moments belong alongside difficulties.

A stronger partnership

Clear boundaries

Understand what information is held, who can see it and how it is used.

Your information. Your child.

People remain in control.

See My Mind is designed so the right information is available only to the right people. Technology can help people see patterns more clearly. It does not diagnose children or make decisions about them.

Please do not include names or identifying information about individual children when you first contact us.
