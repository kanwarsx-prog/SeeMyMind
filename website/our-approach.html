Our approach

Technology should help people notice more, not care less.

See My Mind is built around lived experience, classroom reality and the belief that children’s information deserves extraordinary care.

What we believe

Good support starts with understanding.

The hardest part of inclusive education is rarely a lack of effort. It is that the important pieces of a child’s day are distributed across people, places and systems.

We are building the connective tissue that lets those pieces become useful — while keeping judgement, relationships and responsibility with people.

01

Evidence before assumption

Insights should be traceable to what a child, family or professional has actually shared.

02

Guidance, never diagnosis

NOYA can surface patterns and suggest possibilities. It does not label children or make decisions.

03

Access by role

Parents, children, staff and leaders need different views, with clear boundaries around sensitive information.

04

Strengths belong in the record

A useful picture includes what helps a child flourish, not only what went wrong.

The founding principle

Every child who comes after ours should grow up in a world where the puzzle was already solved.
