Our mission

Every child deserves to be understood, not simply managed.

See My Mind began with one child being seen differently by the people around him. We are building a way for the people who care to work from the same picture.

The beginning

“What if everyone supporting a child could work from the same picture?”

Shy founded See My Mind after seeing her own child experience many of the same challenges Luca represents — being understood differently at home and at school. She knew what helped, but that understanding did not always reach the people who needed it.

Everyone cared and everyone was trying to help. Nobody had the whole picture. See My Mind grew from that gap.

What we believe

Principles for a more useful kind of support.

Understood, not just managed

Every child deserves adults who ask why, not only what happened.

Behaviour is communication

A bad night, a worry or a change at home can change what a moment in class really means.

Needs, not labels

A child is more than an incident, a diagnosis or a support plan.

Strengths, not deficits

What a child loves, achieves and is proud of matters as much as what they find hard.

The child’s own voice

Children should help tell their own story, not have it told entirely for them.

Understanding should build

Useful knowledge should not disappear when a teacher, class or school changes.

Technology and people

Technology supports people. It never replaces them.

See My Mind can join up information, spot patterns and make evidence easier to see. The decisions about a child belong to the people who know and care for them.

We start with SEND/SEMH because fragmented information can have serious consequences. The vision is every child.

Our founding principle

No child should have to reach crisis before the people around them understand what they need.
